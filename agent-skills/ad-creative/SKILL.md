# Ad creative

1. **Start from evidence.** Read which of the client's ads carry spend at a good CPA, and look at competitors' long-running ads in the ad library tools. Long-running usually means profitable.
2. **Write the concept first.** For each asset: the angle, the hook in the first line or first 3 seconds, the visual, the offer and the call to action. Get the concept right before spending on generation.
3. **Make variants that test one thing.** Change the hook, the visual or the offer, not all three at once, so the results teach something.
4. **Meet the specs.** Meta feed 1:1 or 4:5, stories and reels 9:16, with text kept out of the outer 14% at top and bottom. Taboola thumbnails 16:9 with no text-heavy images. Keep text on images short.
5. **Check before handing back.** Score each image with the scoring tool where one exists, and check spelling, brand colours, logos and claims against the brief. Reject anything with mangled text, extra fingers or a fake-looking product.
6. Generation runs cost money, so keep runs to the variants the card asks for. Launching creative in an ad account is Tier 2: hand back the assets and the proposed ads, and the owner approves.
