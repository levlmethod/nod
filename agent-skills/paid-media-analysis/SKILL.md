# Paid media analysis

Work top-down: account, campaign, ad set, ad, then creative.

## Metrics

- **Spend and pacing**: spend against the client's budget and against the previous period. Under-delivery is as much a problem as overspend.
- **Efficiency**: CPC, CPM, CTR, CPA and ROAS. Judge CPA and ROAS on the client's real conversion event, not on clicks.
- **Delivery health**: frequency over about 3 in a week on prospecting means fatigue. A learning-limited ad set or a disapproved ad means delivery trouble, not audience trouble.
- **Native (Taboola)**: watch site and placement breakdowns. A few publisher sites often eat budget at a poor CPA; block lists and bid adjustments by site are the usual fix.

## Order of checks

1. Did spend move? Look for budget edits, paused campaigns, billing problems or disapprovals.
2. Did cost move (CPM or CPC)? Look at auction pressure, seasonality, audience size and creative fatigue.
3. Did conversion rate move? Compare the platform's conversions with GA4 or Stripe for the same days to split a tracking break from a real drop, then check the landing page.
4. Which creative is carrying the account? Rank ads by spend and CPA. Name the fatigued ones.

## Proposals

You read and propose. A budget or bid change, launching, resuming or turning on ads is Tier 2, even when it lowers a budget: put the exact change, its expected effect and the risk in the card's next action, and the owner approves it. Pausing an ad, ad set or campaign only cuts spend, so it's Tier 1, but say why in the card's evidence. When you signed in through a browser profile, use it to read unless the card's next step is a Tier 1 pause.
