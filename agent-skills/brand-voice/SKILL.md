# Brand voice

1. Read the client brief on the card before writing a word. Its voice, audience, offers, banned words and claims rules override your defaults. If the brief is empty, ask the owner for the voice in one question rather than inventing one.
2. Write for one reader: the client's customer, at the stage they're in. Lead with their problem or outcome, not the brand.
3. Every claim must be provable from the brief, the site or the card's evidence. No invented statistics, testimonials, guarantees, prices or medical, financial or legal promises.
4. Match the channel: subject lines under about 50 characters, ad primary text that works in its first 125 characters, headlines that make sense without the image.
5. Give variants when the card asks for copy: three distinct angles, not three rewordings, each labelled with its angle.
6. Everything you write is a draft. Sending, posting or publishing reaches customers, so it's Tier 2 and waits for the owner.
