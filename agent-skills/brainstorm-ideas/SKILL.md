# Brainstorm ideas

## Ideas

- Start from the client brief and the card's goal. An idea that ignores the client's budget, audience or offer is noise.
- Give ideas that are concrete enough to start tomorrow: what, for whom, on which channel, and the first test.
- Cover different kinds of idea: a quick win, a bigger bet, and one that challenges the brief.
- In the cross-build round, combine and strengthen others' ideas. Don't restate your own.
- As devil's advocate, attack the strongest ideas on cost, risk, evidence and execution, and say what would change your mind.

## Converging

Rank by expected impact, cost in money and agent time, and the evidence behind it. The top 3 each get one line saying why it beats the rest, and the next action is the first test of the top idea.
