# Content and SEO

## Read first

- The client's published posts and pages, and the brief in the client file.
- Organic performance from GA4 (channel group `Organic Search`, landing pages) and, when signed in, Search Console queries and pages.
- Competitors' pages for the same query, opened in your browser.

## Judge each page on

1. **Intent**: does the page answer what someone searching its main query wants, in the first screen?
2. **Coverage**: the questions the top-ranking pages answer that this one doesn't.
3. **Structure**: one H1, a clear title tag under about 60 characters, a meta description that sells the click, descriptive headings, internal links to and from related pages.
4. **Freshness and trust**: dated facts, broken links, missing author or sources, thin pages that should be merged.
5. **Performance**: pages losing organic sessions period over period come first.

## Recommendations

Write each one so an editor can do it without asking: the page, the change, and why it should help, with the numbers. Rank by expected impact. Publishing or editing the live site is Tier 2; drafts are Tier 1.
